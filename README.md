# Roof Real Estate - Trifold Brochure Mockup

This repository contains the layout and content assets for the official marketing trifold brochure of **Roof Real Estate**. The design showcases a comprehensive view of both the inner spreads and outer flaps of the print asset.

---

## 🏠 Brand & Project Profile

* **Company Name:** Roof Real Estate
* **Core Value Statement:** "Discover a perfect balance between comfort, style, and serenity."
* **Target Audience:** Families, first-time homebuyers, and property investors.

---

## 📖 Brochure Structure & Content Breakdown

### 1. External Panel / Cover Layout (Bottom Design)
The exterior layout establishes the primary brand image with high-contrast architectural photography and bold value propositions.

* **Front Cover Panel:**
  * **Logo:** Minimalist golden line-art house icon.
  * **Headlines:** `ROOF REAL ESTATE` / `FIND YOUR PERFECT HOME TODAY`.
* **Main Spread Panels (Promotional):**
  * Displays a premium modern residential villa with exterior lighting.
  * **Promo Header:** `SPECIAL PROMOTION THIS MONTH!`
  * **Feature Highlights (Iconography Perks):**
    1. *Free kitchen set & air conditioning units*
    2. *Zero booking fee for early buyers*
    3. *Free notary & legal documents*

---

### 2. Internal Panels / Information Spread (Top Design)
The inner layout uses a clean white grid with teal headers and accents to break down specific offerings.

#### Panel A: "Made For Families"
Focuses on property features tailored for family living, paired with a modern multi-story residential building graphic:
* **Spacious living room**
* **Friendly & safe area**
* **Close to parks & schools**
* **Community-focused neighborhood**

#### Panel B: "Why Choose Us?"
A dedicated block for company positioning and proof points (currently utilizes a structured Latin *Lorem Ipsum* typographic layout block).

#### Panel C: "Book Now" / Call to Action (CTA)
A sleek, spacious kitchen interior image leads directly into the localized contact and booking metrics:
* 📍 **Address:** 12, CMR Road, Madurai - 625009
* 📞 **Phone:** +91-823-456-7890 (or country standard variation)
* ✉️ **Email:** a1realestate@gmail.com

## 🎨 Design & Print Specifications

* **Palette:**
  * **Primary:** Deep Teal / Slate Blue (`#1B6B83` approx.)
  * **Accent:** Golden Yellow / Warm White light streaks
  * **Neutral Backgrounds:** True White and Light Grey textures
* **Typography:** Geometric sans-serif headlines for a crisp, contemporary aesthetic.
* **Layout Style:** Trifold template (Standard 8.5" x 11" or A4 equivalent dimension scale).
